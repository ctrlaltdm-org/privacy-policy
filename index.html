Privacy Policy

Developer: CtrlAltDM (CADM) · App: EggDropXperiments (com.ctrlaltdm.eggdropxperiments)
Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Audience: Ages 13 and older

CtrlAltDM (“we”, “our”, “us”) built EggDropXperiments as a free physics puzzle game with optional support features (an optional in-app donation and an optional rewarded advertisement). This page explains what data may be processed when you use the app.

1. Data We Collect Directly

We do not require account registration and we do not operate our own user-tracking servers. Game progress and settings (such as unlocked levels and audio/display preferences) are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services Used

The app integrates the following Google services, which may collect and process device and usage data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Billing (for optional in-app donations such as “Donate $1 for a coffee”): processes purchase transactions, purchase tokens, and order history through Google Play. We do not receive your payment card or banking details. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Payments Privacy Notice.
  • Google AdMob (for optional “Watch an Ad” rewarded video ads): may collect device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID), IP address / approximate location, app interaction events, crash logs, and diagnostics to serve, cap, and measure advertisements and prevent fraud. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Advertising Choices & Consent

Rewarded advertisements are strictly optional and only shown when you choose to watch an ad. You can reset or limit your Android Advertising ID at any time in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Where required by regional law (such as the EEA/UK), Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) or device-level settings govern ad consent.

4. Age & Children’s Privacy

EggDropXperiments is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Data Deletion & Retention

Local game data can be deleted at any time by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Data processed by Google Play or Google AdMob is retained and governed according to Google’s retention policies.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
